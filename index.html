Prime Focus Timer

Android Platform Version 1.1.7 Productivity

Prime Focus Timer is a productivity application designed to help users manage focus sessions, organize activities, build consistent routines, and make better use of their time.

Focus Sessions & Breaks

Structured intervals with customizable work-to-rest cadences.

Collections & Custom Timers

Organize routines into tailored tags, categories, and presets.

Time Laps & Activity Routines

Track granular session segments with seamless state retention.

Scheduling & Notifications

Non-intrusive sound cues and local notification alerts.

Productivity Analytics

Visual weekly progress logs, completion rates, and streaks.

Offline-First Functionality

100% device-stored data with zero server tracking or ads injection.
